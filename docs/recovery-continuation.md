# Funded continuation invariants

Existing downstream and automatic recovery history is append-only. A successor names its immediate predecessor and immutable task, candidate, and source hashes. It cannot fork an active chain or reset history through a different model, run, price, prompt, or unrelated English commit.

Historical version 1 requests retain their once-per-source rule. Accepted version 2 lineages count at most three cycles per language/article/English content fingerprint and at most two per applicable strategy, including earlier accepted cycles. Automatic version 3 lineages can continue within the same finite authority only when substantive progress is demonstrated. Actual accepted history remains counted after cancellation or partial staging.

A strategy identity includes actual generation/review prompts, selected model/reasoning settings, language guidance, glossary, output limits, and acceptance threshold. Automatic successors observe the existing one-hour cooldown. Unchanged or cyclic candidates, repeated substantive defects, ambiguous progress, refusals, unknown outcomes, source changes, human intervention, and exhausted limits stop for attention. A score alone does not prove progress.

Funding covers the complete repair/translation plus independent review chain before either is submitted. Original envelopes and reservations are permanent history. Any permitted settlement requires complete terminal evidence; unknown or partial charges retain their reservation. Larger complete-candidate limits do not grant more spending authority, and oversized text is retained rather than truncated.

New ordinary recovery uses the [plain translation policy](plain-translation-runtime.md) under the existing cumulative automatic authority. The specialized repair workflow and exact-manual-recovery interface are removed. Existing funded downstream campaigns continue through the collector with their exact recorded source/model/budget contracts. Human-edited pairs remain permanently protected.

Reports distinguish unfinished candidates from accepted publications and preserve current source freshness. A stopped campaign is not necessarily successful. Failed upgrades keep their last accepted public version. Tests verify lineage, funding, Batch reconciliation, and publication protection offline; they do not certify model quality or authorize another allocation.
